# PenDraw

PenDraw turns your iPhone into a motion-controlled pen.
Draw freely in the air and see your strokes appear on screen in real time.

## How It Works
PenDraw uses the device’s motion sensors to track movement and convert it into drawing input on the screen.

## Privacy
PenDraw does not collect, store, or share any personal data.
All motion data is processed locally on the device.
